CSCI 3753 Operating Systems · CU Boulder

Base Camp Academy

Lecture notes you can read on your own or present in class, and the Friday recitations that go with them. Each lecture reads like a tutorial, with hands-on experiments running in your browser. Lectures are numbered L and recitations R. Lab guides walk you through each Basecamp lab. Quizzes, problem sets and exams are taken on Canvas. The Spring 2027 schedule lists every week.
