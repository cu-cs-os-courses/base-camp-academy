CSCI 3753 Operating Systems · CU Boulder · Spring 2027

The schedule

This page lists, week by week, the lectures, the recitations, and the Sundays on which each quiz, problem set, and lab opens and is due. Lectures are on Tuesday and Thursday, and recitation is on Friday. Canvas shows the exact time of every deadline.

The weekly rhythm

  • Sunday is the switch. Every quiz, problem set, and lab opens and closes on a Sunday. There are no midweek deadlines. A Sunday often closes one assignment and opens the next.
  • Tuesday and Thursday are lectures. Each lecture lists its chapters of the textbook, Silberschatz, Galvin, and Gagne, Operating System Concepts, 10th edition. Read them before or after the lecture. The quizzes assume that you did.
  • Friday is recitation. It works one example on the week's material, and then helps with the current lab.
